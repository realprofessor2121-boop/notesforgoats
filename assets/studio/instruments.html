FEEL YOUR MIX
Drag the top to move

Play the feeling.

Start: press Enable sound, then touch a color or piano key. Drag across pads or keys to glide. Multiple fingers can play together. Play groove starts a built-in four-chord demonstration. Stop or Escape silences all voices and effects.

Computer keys: 1–7 play Red through Pink. A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play a chromatic octave starting at C4. + Oct / − Oct move that octave. Space toggles the groove unless you are using a slider or button.

Mix: each color has its own volume. M mutes; S solos. Master controls everything; Piano volume controls every sound selected in the piano menu. Open the mix box to change EQ, compression, reverb and stereo pan. Sliders change the real audio signal.

Notes: your reference image supplies the original character. The Notes ↗ button keeps a small, draggable character view visible when the main stage is off screen. This is an audio-reactive 2D demonstration, not a replacement Blender rig. Body points are artistic NFG controls; they do not claim that a note or instrument acts on a particular body part.

Tuning: all piano keys use f = 440 × 2^((MIDI − 69) / 12). A4 is 440.00 Hz, C4 is 261.63 Hz. The number is the note's fundamental, not every harmonic in an instrument. Drum and noise pads do not have a single fixed note frequency. Scale guides color the seven degrees in NFG order; dimmed chromatic notes are still playable.

Local & lightweight: this one HTML file includes the character image and synth engine. No accounts, microphone, external libraries or network requests are needed to play. Mixer settings can be remembered by this browser where local storage is available. An imported sound is session-only. This file does not store or alter the original NFG project.
